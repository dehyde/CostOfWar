# Researching Channel 14 Claims

## Core rule

Treat the factual claim, not an article criticizing Channel 14, as the unit of analysis. Capture the closest original Channel 14 wording/media and verify it against strong evidence independently.

Read `channel14-factcheck/LIBRARY_CONFIG.md` before writing.

## Workflow

1. Discover candidate claims from Channel 14 broadcasts/site/YouTube/social, reputable fact-checking/watchdog reporting, corrections, official responses and issue-specific research.
2. Recover the original wording/clip. If only a secondary paraphrase exists, keep as candidate until the original is found or explicitly note the limitation.
3. Record `Claim Origin`: distinguish presenter/editor, reporter, graphic/chyron, recurring panelist, guest/interviewee, external claim repeated by Channel 14, or unclear.
4. Decide whether the statement is fact-checkable. Exclude pure opinion, rhetoric, satire and predictions unless they contain a separable factual assertion.
5. Search for the strongest direct evidence. Social posts alone cannot establish the verdict.
6. Prefer official records/data, courts, primary documents, state findings, reputable independent reporting with identifiable sourcing, and strong research evidence.
7. For high-materiality claims, seek two independent evidence routes when practical.
8. Apply the narrowest verdict: False, Misleading, Unsupported, Missing context, Outdated, Unverifiable, or Accurate/no issue.
9. Preserve uncertainty and important contrary evidence. Do not force a negative verdict.
10. Deduplicate repeated versions of the same factual claim before insertion.
11. Write canonical Claim + Sources before publishing the visual card.

## Completion checks

- exact or responsibly reconstructed claim wording;
- origin correctly attributed;
- claim is factual/checkable;
- verdict addresses the central assertion, not a strawman;
- at least one strong non-social counter-evidence source for negative verdicts;
- material contrary evidence noted;
- original media candidate linked when available;
- no duplicate canonical claim.
