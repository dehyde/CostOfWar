# Expanding Channel 14 Fact-Check Library

**REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** Use `researching-channel14-claims` for claim verification and verdicts.

**REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** Use `publishing-factcheck-media` for exact media and gallery publication.

Read `channel14-factcheck/LIBRARY_CONFIG.md` first.

## Batch workflow

1. Define time window/search focus.
2. Discover candidates broadly but keep them separate from accepted claims.
3. Prioritize claims with recoverable original Channel 14 media and meaningful public impact.
4. Resolve exact wording/origin and deduplicate.
5. Gather credible counter-evidence; social posts alone never establish a negative verdict.
6. Write Claims and Sources to the canonical Sheet.
7. Recover original media, generate stable exact preview and write Media.
8. Publish/update Notion from canonical values.
9. Re-query Sheet and Notion. Do not report completion if evidence, preview, origin or verdict fields disagree.

Never force a negative result. A candidate may be rejected as accurate/no issue, unverifiable or non-factual.
