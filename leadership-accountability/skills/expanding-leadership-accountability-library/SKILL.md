# Expanding Leadership Accountability Library

**REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** Use `researching-leadership-accountability` for evidence classification and verification.

**REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** Use `publishing-accountability-media` for exact media and gallery publication.

Read `leadership-accountability/LIBRARY_CONFIG.md` first.

## Batch workflow

1. Define time window and subjects; start with the canonical People list.
2. Discover candidates across all accountability categories, not only scandal/legal stories.
3. Prioritize showcaseable exact media while keeping strong document-only events.
4. Resolve event identity, responsibility link, legal status and subject response.
5. Deduplicate before insertion.
6. Write Events + Sources in the canonical Sheet.
7. Recover exact media and stable preview; write Media.
8. Publish/update Notion from canonical data.
9. Re-query affected records and verify category, legal status, response/defense and media.

Do not force a negative interpretation. Reject candidates where responsibility, factual contradiction, conflict, misconduct or legal status cannot be responsibly established.
