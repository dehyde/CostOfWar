# Pre-Oct 7 Warnings Library

## Scope
Document distinct warnings **given before 2023-10-07** to members of Israel's serving government or senior state/security decision-makers about an impending attack, severe security failure, strategic deterioration, border vulnerability, readiness collapse, regional escalation, or another major disaster materially relevant to October 7 preparedness.

Exclude warnings originating from Israeli opposition politicians/parties acting as political rivals. Include professional bodies, serving/former security officials acting in a professional capacity, IDF/intelligence bodies, foreign governments/diplomats, researchers/academics, expert organizations, local authorities, emergency/security professionals, and other non-opposition sources.

## Temporal rule
The **warning event** must predate October 7, 2023. Evidence establishing or clarifying that warning may have been published **before or after October 7**. Later evidence can include State Comptroller material, commissions/inquiries, sworn testimony, court records, declassified or later-released intelligence, official correspondence released later, government/security acknowledgments, and high-quality investigative reporting that reproduces or independently corroborates contemporaneous records.

Always record the warning date separately from the evidence publication/release date. Never reject strong evidence merely because it surfaced after October 7.

## Unit
One distinct warning event. Repeated retellings of the same warning are evidence attached to that event, not separate events.

## Required fields
- warning date/time or best-known range
- evidence publication/release date(s) and whether evidence is contemporaneous, retrospective, or both
- warning source/person/organization + professional role
- recipient(s)
- government-level receipt: yes/no/unclear
- receipt evidence: direct documented / strongly documented / reported / unclear
- exact warning or narrow paraphrase
- threat/disaster type
- specificity: Specific / Moderately specific / General strategic
- geographic/operational scope
- documented government response: Acted / Partially acted / Rejected-dismissed / No documented action / Unclear
- what happened afterward, stated descriptively without causation inference
- primary evidence and corroborating sources
- contradictions, denials, limiting facts, uncertainty and corrections
- confidence: High / Medium / Low

## Evidence rules
1. Prefer contemporaneous documents, recordings, letters, meeting records, messages, official assessments, military/intelligence records, foreign-government records and other primary evidence.
2. Later evidence is acceptable when it reliably establishes a pre-Oct. 7 warning.
3. A retrospective claim that someone "warned them" is not enough by itself. Require contemporaneous evidence or meaningful independent corroboration.
4. Distinguish an **internal security warning** from a **government warning**. Do not say the government was warned unless escalation to a political/government recipient is evidenced.
5. Never infer that a warning was "ignored" merely because October 7 occurred. Record only the documented response or lack of documented response.
6. Never infer motive or causation.
7. Preserve government/official denials, defenses, contrary assessments, conflicting timelines and exculpatory facts.
8. Distinguish a specific Hamas/Gaza attack warning from broader warnings about deterrence erosion, readiness degradation, border vulnerability, internal-security degradation, regional-war risk or strategic disaster.

## Source priority
Primary official evidence first; then authoritative later findings and Reuters/AP, Kan, N12, Ynet, Haaretz, Times of Israel, Guardian and other high-quality reporting; specialist academic/research work may corroborate or identify primary sources.

## Backfill scope
Systematically cover **2022-12-29 through 2023-10-06 month by month and source lane by source lane**.

Also maintain an **older-warning lane with no arbitrary historical floor** when:
- a warning chain began before the current government took office;
- an older warning was repeated or explicitly delivered to current officeholders; or
- older professional/official assessments materially establish inherited documented knowledge of the risk.

Older-warning work runs concurrently with the primary window when strong leads exist; it is not deferred until the recent window is complete.

## Source lanes
1. IDF / Military Intelligence / Southern Command / Gaza Division / surveillance units / reserve-security professionals
2. Shin Bet / Mossad / National Security Council / police / Home Front / other Israeli professional security bodies
3. Foreign governments / diplomats / foreign intelligence-security services / regional partners
4. Professional experts / academics / researchers / think tanks / political scientists / nonpartisan security institutes
5. Local authorities / Gaza-border communities / security coordinators / emergency-infrastructure professionals / civil professional organizations
6. Primary-document recovery + historical blind spots, including later-released post-Oct. 7 evidence
7. Older inherited/repeated warnings

## Hourly allocation
- 15% freshness/document upgrades across accepted and unresolved warnings
- 25% unresolved-first evidence resolution
- 50% exactly one rotating deep-discovery lane, persisted in `RUN_STATE.md`
- 10% dedupe, Notion publication/upgrade, source-quality audit and state update

Do not spend two consecutive deep-discovery runs on the same lane unless `RUN_STATE.md` records a specific high-value primary-document lead that warrants continuation.

## Publication
Canonical publication layer: Notion database `Pre-Oct 7 Warnings`.
Each accepted warning gets one rich Notion entry containing chronology, exact warning content, recipient evidence, response, contemporaneous and retrospective evidence, limiting/contrary evidence, source assessment and references.

Candidate, coverage and rotation state remain in GitHub.

## Completeness
Track both **month × source-lane coverage** and the older-warning lane. A month is not Complete merely because famous cases were found. Mark `No results` only after targeted searching and `Blocked` only when access/provenance prevents resolution. Never mark a period Complete until every required lane is Complete, No results, or explicitly Blocked with its gap recorded.
