# Pre-Oct 7 Warnings Library

## Scope
Document warnings issued before 2023-10-07 to members of the serving Israeli government or senior state/security decision-makers concerning an impending attack, severe security failure, strategic deterioration, or major disaster.

Exclude warnings originating from Israeli opposition politicians/parties acting as political rivals. Include professional bodies, serving/former security officials acting professionally, IDF/intelligence bodies, foreign governments/diplomats, researchers/academics, expert organizations, civil-society/professional organizations, local authorities, emergency/security professionals, and other non-opposition sources.

## Unit
One distinct warning event.

## Required fields
- date/time or best-known range
- warning source/person/organization + professional role
- recipient(s)
- whether receipt is directly documented, strongly documented, reported, or unclear
- exact warning or narrow paraphrase
- threat/disaster type
- specificity: Specific / Moderately specific / General strategic
- geographic/operational scope
- documented government response or lack of documented response
- what happened afterward, stated descriptively without causation inference
- primary evidence and all corroborating sources
- contradictions, denials, uncertainty, corrections
- confidence: High / Medium / Low

## Evidence rules
1. Prefer contemporaneous pre-2023-10-07 documents, recordings, letters, meeting records, sworn testimony, official correspondence, intelligence/military documents, government/foreign-government records, or later official reproductions of such records.
2. A post-event claim that someone 'warned them' is not enough by itself. Require contemporaneous evidence or meaningful independent corroboration.
3. Never infer that a warning was ignored merely because October 7 occurred. Record the response as documented: Acted / Partially acted / Rejected-dismissing / No documented action / Unclear.
4. Never infer motive or causation.
5. Preserve government/official denials, defenses, limiting facts, conflicting timelines, and uncertainty.
6. Distinguish specific attack warnings from broader strategic warnings such as deterrence erosion, readiness degradation, border vulnerability, regional-war risk, and internal-security degradation.
7. Deduplicate repeated retellings of the same warning; attach new evidence to the existing warning record.

## Source priority
Primary official evidence first; then Reuters/AP, Kan, N12, Ynet, Haaretz, Times of Israel, Guardian, other high-quality reporting, and specialist research where relevant.

## Coverage
Primary systematic window: 2022-12-29 through 2023-10-06. After that window is systematically resolved, add older high-salience warnings that materially bear on the same government's October 7 preparedness.

## Publication
Canonical publication layer: Notion database `Pre-Oct 7 Warnings`.
Each accepted warning gets one rich Notion entry. Candidate/coverage state remains in GitHub.

## Completeness
Never mark a period Complete unless all required source lanes are resolved or explicitly Blocked with gaps.
